## Problem

Prometheus 3.0 is an avatar humanoid robot — the first in Mexico — built by
[Inbiodroid](https://inbiodroid.com), a finalist company in the **ANA Avatar
XPRIZE**. For autonomous navigation, the robot needed an accurate map of its
surroundings.

## My role

**Embedded engineer intern** and the youngest member of a 10-person
engineering team.

## Approach

- **Designed and integrated a LiDAR-based SLAM system** for autonomous
  navigation.
- During the internship, **co-invented a telepresence control system**
  (Mexican patent pending).

## Results

- SLAM system achieving **under 5 cm mapping accuracy**.
- The telepresence control system **improved response latency by about 20%**.
- Presented Prometheus 3.0 to **government officials**.
